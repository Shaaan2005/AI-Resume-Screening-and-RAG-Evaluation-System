# Retrieval Benchmark Results

Generated by `python benchmark/run_benchmark.py`. 48 labeled recruiter queries (2 per job category) against 242 resumes (1092 chunks) from `resumes/`.

A result is **relevant** when its dataset category matches the query's category. Retrieval is measured over the whole index with no category filter.

- **P@5 / P@10**: share of the top 5 / 10 results that are relevant
- **MRR**: 1 / rank of the first relevant result, averaged
- **nDCG@10**: ranking quality of the top 10 (1.0 = perfect)
- **Latency**: median per query on the machine that ran the benchmark

## Caveats

- Category labels are a proxy for relevance: a strong Finance resume can be a fair answer to an Accounting query but counts as a miss here.
- 48 queries is small; differences of a few points between configurations are within noise.
- Category labels cannot measure ranking *within* a department (e.g. Python vs Java engineers), which is where a re-ranker could still help. That needs skill-level labels.
