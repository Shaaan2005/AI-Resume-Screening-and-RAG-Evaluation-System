04 Pipeline

How it works

STEP 01

Department detection

A cross-encoder reads the job description and picks the department to search.

STEP 02

Keyword match

Checks which skills the job asks for appear in the resume.

STEP 03

Semantic similarity

Scores every section of the resume against the job; the best section decides the verdict.

STEP 04

Vector search

Ranks the closest resumes in the index by cosine similarity.
